About this prototype

This clickable low-fidelity concept illustrates the candidate retail-banking journeys in the case study.

  • Sign-in to unified product view
  • Account and statement access
  • Transfer preparation and review
  • Credit-card statements and payment
  • Utility biller selection

Prototype boundary: This is a front-end demonstration. It does not authenticate, connect to bank systems, or initiate payments.

Balance values, transaction rules, statement definitions, security controls, and support policies remain placeholders pending stakeholder approval.
