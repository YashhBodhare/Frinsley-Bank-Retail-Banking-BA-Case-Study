# Stakeholder Questionnaire — Digital Banking and Card Servicing

**Purpose:** Capture needs and risks for the proposed customer-facing banking and credit-card modules. This is a draft instrument. No responses or survey results are included.

## A. Respondent context

1. Which role best describes you? (Customer / Product / Branch or contact centre / Payments operations / Card operations / Technology / Security or fraud / Risk, compliance or legal / Other)
2. Which areas do you work with? (Accounts / Statements / Transfers / Utility payments / Credit cards / Customer support / Technology and controls / Other)
3. Which customer/channel types should this release support? (Open response)

## B. Customer needs and current experience

4. What are the three most important tasks a customer should be able to complete digitally? (Select up to 3: view accounts; view statements; transfer money; pay a utility bill; view credit card; pay card balance; get help; other)
5. What is difficult, slow, unclear, or error-prone in the current process? (Open response)
6. How often do customers contact staff for these tasks? (Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Rarely / Unknown)
7. What information should be visible in the unified view? (Open response)

## C. Functional scope

8. Which statement views are required? (Current period / Previous period / Detailed transaction view / Download or print / Other)
9. What details must appear for an account and for a credit card? (Open response)
10. Which transfer types and use cases are required? (NEFT / RTGS / IMPS / Internal transfer / Other; note applicable geography and rules)
11. What should happen before a transfer is submitted? (Beneficiary selection, details review, fee disclosure, authentication, confirmation, other)
12. Which utility payment categories or billers are required for the first release? (Open response)
13. How should customers pay a credit-card balance? (Full balance / Minimum due / Other amount / Scheduled payment / Other; validate policy)

## D. Exceptions, controls, and quality

14. What should the customer see for pending, rejected, timed-out, duplicated, reversed, or partially completed transactions?
15. What authentication or additional verification is required for sign-in and sensitive actions?
16. What audit, privacy, data retention, accessibility, language, availability, and performance needs should be considered?
17. Which customer situations need special handling? (Joint accounts, blocked cards, dormant accounts, disputes, limits, failed biller response, other)
18. What support path should be offered when a task cannot be completed?

## E. Prioritization and validation

19. Rate each candidate feature: Must have / Should have / Could have / Not in initial release. (Account details; unified view; statements; transfer; utility payment; card details; card statements; card balance payment; support)
20. What is the most important release risk or unresolved policy decision? (Open response)
21. Who should approve the requirements and business rules for your area? (Role/name)

## Analysis and reporting template

After real responses are collected, report respondent count, role mix, collection dates, question-level counts, themes, conflicting views, and limitations. Do not fabricate a response sample or imply the instrument has been administered.
